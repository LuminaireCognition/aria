## Waves/Rooms

### Pocket 1 - War Zone
Two faction groups; no initial aggression until attacked. Drones safe to deploy.

**Angel Group** (gate trigger: destroy Gist War General)
- 4x Elite Frigate Arch Gistii Impaler/Hunter (warp disrupt, web)
- 1x Elite Cruiser Arch Gistum Smasher/Crusher (target paint)
- 5x Battlecruiser Gistatis Primus/Tribuni
- 3x Battleship Gist War General/General/Commander **[Gate Trigger]**

**Sansha Group** (gate trigger: destroy Centus Savage Lord)
- 3x Elite Frigate Centii Loyal Ravener/Scavenger (warp disrupt, web)
- 3x Battlecruiser Centatis Specter/Wraith
- 4x Battleship Centus Savage Lord/Overlord/Beast Lord/Dark Lord **[Gate Trigger]**

### Pocket 2A - Angel Base
All ships must be destroyed to unlock gate to Pocket 3. Group 1 auto-aggros.

**Group 1 (Auto-Aggro, 2-3km)**
- 2x Frigate Angel Viper (Spy) (web) - shooting with guns/missiles does not aggro others; avoid using drones on Spies
- 3x Sentry Angel Sentry Guns (target paint)

**Group 2**
- 3x Battlecruiser Gistatis Praefectus/Tribuni
- 2x Battleship Gist Saint/Warlord

**Group 3 (20-30km)**
- 5x Destroyer Gistior Seizer/Thrasher
- 3x Elite Cruiser Arch Gistum Phalanx/Centurion

**Group 4 (20-30km)**
- 6x Elite Frigate Arch Gistii Thug/Outlaw (warp disrupt, web)
- 4x Battleship Gist General/Commander

**Random Spawn**
- 1x Battleship Angel Overseer

### Pocket 2B - Sansha Base
All ships must be destroyed to unlock gate to Pocket 3. Group 1 auto-aggros.

**Group 1 (Auto-Aggro)**
- 2x Frigate Sansha Spy (web)

**Group 2**

### Pocket 3 - Research Outpost
Warp-in ~5km from Heron. Group 1 auto-aggros; other groups triggered by proximity. Always Angel ships regardless of entry point. Damage profile: 50-63% Explosive, 2-31% Thermal, 6-23% Kinetic.

**Group 1 (Auto-Aggro)**
- 3x Elite Cruiser Arch Gistum Phalanx/Centurion
- 2x Battleship Gist War General/Warlord

**Group 2**
- 4x Battlecruiser Gistatis Legionnaire/Primus
- 1x Battleship Gist Warlord/Malakim

**Group 3**
- 5x Destroyer Gistior Thrasher/Seizer
- 3x Battleship Gist Saint/Warlord/Malakim

**Group 4**
- 4x Elite Frigate Arch Gistii Hijacker/Rogue (warp disrupt, web)
- 1x Battleship Gist Nephilim/Cherubim

## Blitz
1. Use Zbikoki's Hacker Card to bypass Pocket 1 gate lock (or kill trigger battleships).
2. Destroy all ships in Pocket 2 (required to unlock gate).
3. In Pocket 3, kill Group 1, retrieve crew from Heron, return to agent.

## Tactical Notes
- Two-faction mission: you choose which faction's base to enter from Pocket 1 (Angel or Sansha gate). Both bases must be cleared before Pocket 3.
- Pocket 3 is always Angel NPCs regardless of which path you took.
- Spy frigates: safe to shoot with guns/missiles without drawing group aggro; drones on Spies will aggro the room.
- Set drones to passive in all pockets.
- Omni tank recommended due to mixed faction damage profiles.
