## Waves/Rooms

Single ungated pocket.

### Initial Group (25-44km, Auto-Aggro)
- 1x Sentry Starbase Stasis Tower (web)
- 3-4x Destroyer Centior Abomination/Monster
- 4-5x Battleship Centus Mutant Lord/Savage Lord/Beast Lord **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 1]**

### Reinforcement 1 (3 groups)

**Front (40-45km):**
- 2-3x Elite Cruiser Centum Loyal Beast/Juggernaut/Execrator (tracking disrupt)
- 4x Battlecruiser Centatis Wraith/Devil

**Bottom (65-75km):**
- 2-3x Destroyer Centior Cannibal/Misshape
- 2-3x Elite Cruiser Centum Loyal Fiend/Hellhound (tracking disrupt)

**Rear (70-80km):**
- 3-4x Elite Cruiser Centum Loyal Beast/Juggernaut/Slaughter (tracking disrupt)
- 4-5x Battleship Centus Savage Lord/Beast Lord **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 2]**

### Reinforcement 2 (2 groups)

**Front (50-55km):**
- 1-2x Destroyer Centior Horror/Abomination
- 2-3x Battleship Centus Mutant Lord/Savage Lord

**Rear (85-90km):**
- 1-2x Elite Frigate Centii Loyal Slavehunter/Savage (tracking disrupt)
- 2-3x Battleship Centus Mutant Lord/Overlord/Savage Lord **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 3]**

### Reinforcement 3 (2 groups)

**Group 1:**
- 2-3x Elite Frigate Centii Loyal Slavehunter/Savage (tracking disrupt)
- 2-3x Elite Cruiser Centum Loyal Execrator/Slaughterer (tracking disrupt)
- 1x Battleship Centus Beast Lord/Savage Lord/Overlord **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 4]**

**Bottom (85km):**
- 3-4x Battleship Centus Dark Lord/Overlord

### Reinforcement 4 - Final Wave
- 3-4x Elite Frigate Centii Loyal Scavenger/Minion/Ravener (scram, web)
- 3-4x Battleship Centus Overlord/Dark Lord
- 1x Battleship Centus Tyrant **(mission objective)**
- 0-1x Commander Battleship True Sansha (rare, faction module drops)

## Blitz
Snipe trigger battleships or use a dual-rep perma-tank to advance waves quickly. Destroy the Centus Tyrant in the final wave.

## Tactical Notes
- **Tracking disruptors are extremely heavy** across all waves. Elite cruisers have up to 60km tracking disruptor range.
- Do not use turret battleships; missiles or drones strongly recommended.
- Estimated bounties: ~19.53M ISK. Loot: ~7.69M ISK. Salvage: ~8.52M ISK.
