# The Blockade L4 (DED)
Source: https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/The_Blockade_(DED)_(Level_4)

## Quick Reference
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Faction | DED (CONCORD) |
| Damage Dealt | EM, Thermal, Kinetic, Explosive (omni) |
| Tank | Omni (EM, Thermal, Kinetic, Explosive) |
| Deal | Kinetic, Thermal |
| Objective | Kill all DED Army Generals |
| EWAR | Stasis web, warp scrambler |

## Waves/Rooms

Single ungated pocket.

### Initial Group
- 1x Caldari Stasis Tower (web)
- 4x Cruiser DED Officer 1st Class
- 3x Battleship DED Army Colonel
- 1x Battleship DED Army General **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 1]**

### Reinforcement 1
- 4x Frigate DED Special Ops Panther (web, scrambler)
- 3x Cruiser DED Special Ops Raptor
- 3x Battleship DED Army Colonel
- 1x Battleship DED Army General **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 2]**

### Reinforcement 2
- 5x Frigate DED Special Ops Piranha
- 4x Cruiser DED Army Captain
- 3x Battleship DED Army Colonel
- 1x Battleship DED Army General **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 3]**

### Reinforcement 3
- 3x Cruiser DED Officer 3rd Class
- 3x Battleship DED Army Colonel
- 1x Battleship DED Army General **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 4]**

### Reinforcement 4 - Final Wave
- 4x Frigate DED Special Ops Panther (web, scrambler)
- 4x Battleship DED Army Colonel/General

## Blitz
Kill all DED Army Generals. Prepare for severe incoming damage.

## Tactical Notes
- DED deals **omni damage** -- you need balanced resists across all four damage types.
- DED Special Ops Panthers have web and scrambler; they will pin you down.
- CONCORD Emblem Tags drop from all enemies and have significant trade value (~27.5M ISK total): 5 General, 15 Colonel, 15 Officer, 4 Captain, 3 Raptor, 8 Panther, 5 Piranha tags.
- Estimated ISK loot: ~23M ISK. Salvage: ~1M ISK.
