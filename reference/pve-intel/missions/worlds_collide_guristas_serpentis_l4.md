## Waves/Rooms

### Pocket 1 - Warzone

**Serpentis Group (130-140km)**
- 4x Elite Frigate Guardian Veteran (warp disrupt, web)
- 4x Battlecruiser Corelatis High Captain/Captain Sentry
- 4x Battleship Core Grand High Admiral/Admiral **[Gate Trigger]**

**Guristas Group (120-130km)**
- 4x Battlecruiser Pithatis Revolter/Death Dealer
- 3x Battleship Pith Extinguisher/Dismantler **[Gate Trigger]**

### Pocket 2A - Serpentis Base
Gate locked until all ships destroyed. Group 1 auto-aggros; elite frigates attack drones.

**Group 1**
- 5x Elite Frigate Serpentis Spy/Guardian Veteran (warp disrupt, web)
- 3x Battlecruiser Corelatis Platoon Leader
- 3x Battleship Core Admiral/High Admiral

**Group 2 (Under warp-in)**
- 3x Elite Frigate Guardian Veteran (warp disrupt, web)
- 3x Battlecruiser Corelatis Platoon Leader
- 3x Battleship Core Admiral/High Admiral

**Group 3 (Behind station)**
- 3x Elite Frigate Guardian Veteran (warp disrupt, web)
- 3x Battlecruiser Corelatis Platoon Leader
- 3x Battleship Core Admiral/High Admiral

**Group 4 (Behind station, under Group 3)**
- 1x Battleship Serpentis Overseer (target jammer)

### Pocket 3 - Research Outpost
Group 1 present on arrival. Killing Group 1 unlocks Heron cargo hold for crew retrieval. Contains 32 Veldspar asteroids.

**Group 1**
- 3x Battlecruiser Pithatis Death Dealer/Revolter
- 3x Battleship Pith Extinguisher/Dismantler

**Group 2**
- 3x Battleship Pith Extinguisher/Eliminator (target jammer)

**Group 3**
- 3x Cruiser Pithum Murderer/Ascriber/Silencer/Killer (target jammer)
- 3x Battleship Pith Obliterator/Dismantler

## Blitz
1. Use Zbikoki's Hacker Card to bypass Pocket 1 gate lock (or kill trigger battleships).
2. Clear all ships in chosen Pocket 2 to unlock gate.
3. In Pocket 3, kill Group 1 to unlock Heron cargo hold. Retrieve crew (40 m3) and return to agent.

## Tactical Notes
- Pure Kinetic/Thermal tank is sufficient for both factions.
- Guristas base has heavy ECM (target jammers) on cruisers and overseers; bring ECCM or use drones.
- Serpentis base has many elite frigates with warp disrupt/web across all groups.
- Elite frigates will aggress player drones in all pockets; set drones to passive.
- Attacking lone Guristas Kyoukan frigate may trigger full room aggro.
- Both Serpentis Overseer and Guristas Overseer may not appear immediately on warp-in.
