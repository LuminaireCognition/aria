## Blitz
1. **Pocket 1:** Kill initial ships and Wave 1 triggers to clear the gate.
2. **Pocket 2:** Kill all ships to unlock the gate.
3. **Pocket 3:** Kill only the battleship to unlock the gate (skip sentries).
4. **Pocket 4:** Clear waves via trigger battleships.
5. **Pocket 5:** Kill battleships by highest bounty to trigger waves; destroy Tiogo Kargaz.

## Tactical Notes
- Auto-aggro from initial groups in every pocket.
- Spawn triggers are typically the highest-bounty battleship in each group.
- Elite frigates with webs appear in multiple pockets; fit a web or drones to handle them.
- Pocket 3 can be speed-run by killing only the single battleship.
- Bonus pocket is optional but lucrative (~57.93M ISK total with bounties, loot, and salvage).
- Fleet members can use the bonus gate within 10 seconds of activation without needing their own tag.
- Bring an Angel Diamond Tag if you want to access the bonus pocket.
