## Waves/Rooms

### Initial Spawn (3 groups, Auto-Aggro, 45-55 km)

**Group 1 — Left:**
- 2x Cruiser — EoM Prophet

**Group 2 — Center:**
- 2x Cruiser — EoM Prophet

**Group 3 — Right:**
- 2x Cruiser — EoM Prophet

### Reinforcement Waves (per group — 3 chains total, 4 waves each)

**Wave 1** *(spawns when initial group is killed):*
- 1x Frigate — EoM Demon
- 1x Cruiser — EoM Black Priest *(trigger: Wave 2)*

**Wave 2** *(spawns when Black Priest is destroyed):*
- 1x Frigate — EoM Succubus
- 1x Cruiser — EoM Crusader *(trigger: Wave 3)*

**Wave 3** *(spawns when Crusader is destroyed):*
- 1x Frigate — EoM Succubus
- 1x Cruiser — EoM Crusader *(trigger: Wave 4)*

**Wave 4:**
- 2x Frigate — EoM Demon
- 1x Cruiser — EoM Death Knight

## Blitz
Kill the right spawn, then all Cruisers and Demon Frigates in that reinforcement chain.

## Tactical Notes
- Kill one EoM Prophet from each group first for optimal spawn chaining.
- Approach from left or right side and pick off ships as they approach.
- Total reinforcement spawns possible: 3 complete chains (12 waves total).
