## Waves/Rooms

Single ungated pocket.

### Initial Defenders
- 2x Frigate — Kruul's Henchman
- 4x Cruiser — Pleasure Hub Guardian
- 3x Battleship — Pleasure Hub Sentinel
- 1x Cruiser — Kruul (**trigger**: attacking Kruul spawns reinforcements)

### Kruul's Reinforcements (triggered by attacking Kruul)
- 6x Battleship — Pleasure Hub Sentinel

> Can be avoided by destroying the Pleasure Garden without engaging Kruul.

### Pleasure Garden Wave 1 (75% armor)
- 2x Drone — Spider Drone I (web)
- 4x Cruiser — Pleasure Hub Guardian
- 4x Battleship — Pleasure Hub Sentinel

### Pleasure Garden Wave 2 (40% hull)
- 5x Frigate — Seven Death Dealer
- 3x Cruiser — Seven Assassin
- 1x Battleship — Zor (drops Zbikoki's Hacker Card + implant loot)

## Blitz
Warp in, destroy Kruul's Pleasure Garden (the one **further** from warp-in), loot the Damsel from your cargo hold, return to agent. Avoid shooting Kruul to skip the 6-battleship reinforcement wave.

## Tactical Notes
- **Drone warning:** Do NOT let drones attack Kruul — this triggers the 6x Battleship reinforcement wave.
- **Explosion radius:** Pleasure Garden destruction deals ~3,000 explosive damage in a 30-40 km radius. Recall light/salvage drones before the final blow or they will be destroyed.
- **Webbing:** Spider Drone I units apply webs (spawned at 75% armor on Pleasure Garden).
- **Zor loot:** Zbikoki's Hacker Card allows skipping the first room in Worlds Collide missions. Zor may also drop implants (~100M ISK rare drop).
- **Two gardens:** There are two Pleasure Gardens on grid — destroy the one further from warp-in to complete the mission.
- **Bounties:** ~5.0M ISK. **Loot:** ~7-8M ISK. **Salvage:** ~489K ISK.
