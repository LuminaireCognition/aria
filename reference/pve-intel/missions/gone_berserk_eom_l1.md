## Waves/Rooms

### Initial Spawn (Auto-Aggro)

**Group 1 — Left (~30 km):**
- 2x Frigate — EoM Imp
- 1x Frigate — EoM Fiend *(trigger: Reinforcement 1)*

**Group 2 — Center (~20 km):**
- 2x Frigate — EoM Imp
- 1x Frigate — EoM Fiend *(trigger: Reinforcement 1)*

**Group 3 — Right (~50 km):**
- 2x Frigate — EoM Imp
- 1x Frigate — EoM Fiend *(trigger: Reinforcement 1)*

### Reinforcement Waves (per group — 3 chains total)

**Reinforcement 1** *(spawns when initial Fiend is destroyed):*
- 3x Frigate — EoM Fiend *(each triggers Reinforcement 2)*

**Reinforcement 2** *(spawns per Reinforcement 1 Fiend destroyed):*
- 2x Frigate — EoM Incubus

## Blitz
Destroy the EoM Imp from the right group (as seen from warp-in). Destroy its reinforcements. Warp out.

## Tactical Notes
- Eliminate all Imps first, then kill one Fiend and complete its reinforcement chain before engaging additional Fiends.
- No EWAR documented.
