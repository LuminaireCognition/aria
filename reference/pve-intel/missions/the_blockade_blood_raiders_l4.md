## Waves/Rooms

Single ungated pocket.

### Initial Group (26-28km, Auto-Aggro)
- 1x Sentry Starbase Stasis Tower (web)
- 3-4x Destroyer Corpior Friar/Devoter
- 4-5x Battleship Corpus Apostle/Oracle/Monsignor/Harbinger (neuts) **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 1]**

### Reinforcement 1 (3 groups)

**Front (45-47km):**
- 2-3x Elite Frigate Elder Corpii Diviner/Raider/Reaver
- 2-3x Battlecruiser Corpatis Shade/Fanatic

**Bottom (75-78km):**
- 2-3x Destroyer Corpior
- 2-3x Elite Cruiser Elder Corpum Dark Priest/Shadow Sage

**Rear (80-83km):**
- 3-4x Elite Cruiser Elder Corpum (tracking disrupt)
- 4-5x Battleship Corpus (neuts) **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 2]**

### Reinforcement 2 (2 groups)

**Front (52-54km):**
- 1-3x Destroyer
- 1-2x Battleship Corpus Apostle/Oracle

**Rear (85-90km):**
- 3-4x Elite Frigate Elder Corpii Seeker/Collector (tracking disrupt)
- 4-5x Battleship Corpus (neuts) **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 3]**

### Reinforcement 3 (2 groups)

**Bottom (85-89km):**
- 3-4x Destroyer
- 3-4x Battleship Corpus Cardinal/Monsignor

**Rear (68-70km):**
- 2-3x Destroyer
- 2-3x Elite Cruiser Elder Corpum (tracking disrupt)
- 1x Battleship Corpus Harbinger **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 4]**

### Reinforcement 4 - Final Wave (50-53km)
- 3-4x Elite Frigate Elder Corpii Follower/Worshipper/Herald (scram, web)
- 3-4x Battleship Corpus Cardinal/Monsignor
- 1x Battleship Corpus Pope **(mission objective)**
- 0-1x Commander Dark Blood Battleship (very rare, faction module drops)

## Blitz
Kill trigger battleships in each wave to advance spawns quickly. Final objective is the Corpus Pope in Reinforcement 4.

## Tactical Notes
- **Heavy neut threat** from cruiser-class and battleship-class enemies. Prioritize Elder Corpum cruisers.
- Tracking disruptors are common across multiple waves; missiles or drones recommended.
- Starbase Ship Maintenance Array drops random commodities.
- Estimated bounties: ~20.16M ISK. Loot: ~10.2M ISK. Salvage: ~8.4M ISK.
