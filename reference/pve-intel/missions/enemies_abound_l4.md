## Blitz
- **Part 1:** Clear all NPCs (required to loot objective).
- **Part 2:** Courier — deliver Encoded Data Chip.
- **Part 3:** Kill Group 3 in Pocket 2 (Gallente Navy Orions) for mission completion.
- **Part 4:** Rush to Pocket 3 and destroy the Roden Shipyard Factory Station.
- **Part 5:** Destroy the Smuggler Stargate immediately before reinforcement waves stack up.

## Tactical Notes
- **Parts 1-4 tank Kin/Therm; Part 5 switches to Exp/Kin.** Refit or swap ships between Part 4 and Part 5.
- **Elite frigates with tackle are present in Parts 1, 3, and 4.** Prioritize killing them first to maintain warp-out capability.
- **Remote Sensor Dampening** is common on Gallente battleships (Parts 1, 3). Fit a Sensor Booster or use drones.
- **Part 3 has chained reinforcement triggers.** Avoid attacking the Elite Federation Lixa/Lochos until ready for the next wave.
- **Part 4 Pocket 3** has Roden Shipyard Patrol Officers that fire EM missiles — unusual for Gallente. Consider EM resist if focused by them.
- **Part 5 is time-critical.** New groups spawn every 4 minutes. If you delay, up to 6 groups will be on field simultaneously. Blitz the Smuggler Stargate immediately.
- **Standing loss:** Destroying the Roden Shipyard Factory Station in Part 4 incurs -0.24% Gallente Federation standing.
