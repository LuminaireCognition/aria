## Quick Reference
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Faction | Angel Cartel |
| Damage Dealt | Explosive, Kinetic |
| Tank | Explosive, Kinetic |
| Deal | Explosive, Kinetic |
| Objective | Eliminate the leader of the pirates blockading the stargate |
| EWAR | Stasis webifiers, warp disruptors, target painters |

## Waves/Rooms

Single ungated pocket.

### Initial Group (30km, Auto-Aggro)
- 1x Sentry Starbase Stasis Tower (web)
- 4x Destroyer Gistior Haunter
- 4x Battleship Gist Throne
- 1x Battleship Gist Malakim/Nephilim **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 1]**

### Reinforcement 1 (45-85km, 3 groups)
- 3x Elite Frigate Arch Gistii Ruffian/Nomad (warp disrupt, web)
- 2x Destroyer Gistior Defacer
- 2x Elite Cruiser Arch Gistum Phalanx/Smasher (target paint)
- 2x Battlecruiser Gistatis Praefectus
- 1x Battleship Gist Warlord
- 3x Battleship Gist War General
- 1x Battleship Gist Malakim/Nephilim **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 2]**

### Reinforcement 2 (50-90km)
- 1-2x Elite Frigate Arch Gistii Ruffian (warp disrupt, web)
- 1x Destroyer Gistior Seizer
- 2x Battleship Gist War General/Throne
- 2x Battleship Gist Malakim/Nephilim **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 3]**

### Reinforcement 3 (75-90km)
- 3x Destroyer Gistior Shatterer
- 3x Elite Cruiser Arch Gistum Smasher (target paint)
- 3x Battlecruiser Gistatis Legionnaire
- 3x Battleship Gist Warlord
- 1x Battleship Gist Malakim **[Trigger - spawns Reinforcement 4]**

### Reinforcement 4 - Final Wave
- 3x Elite Frigate Arch Gistii Outlaw (warp disrupt, web)
- 5x Battleship Gist Throne/Malakim/Seraphim
- 0-1x Commander Battleship Gist Domination Commander (very rare, faction module drops)

Mission completes when the Seraphim battleship (highest bounty) is destroyed.

## Blitz
Kill trigger battleships in each wave as fast as possible to reach the final wave, then destroy the Seraphim.

## Tactical Notes
- Set drones to passive to avoid pulling unwanted aggro.
- Destruction Dampening Array can be destroyed but yields no loot.
- Elite frigates with warp disrupt/web appear in multiple waves; be prepared for tackle.
- Target painters from elite cruisers increase incoming damage significantly.
