## Waves/Rooms

### Initial Spawn (3 groups, Auto-Aggro)

**Group 1 — Left:**
- 2x Frigate — EoM Succubus

**Group 2 — Center:**
- 2x Frigate — EoM Succubus

**Group 3 — Right:**
- 2x Frigate — EoM Succubus

### Reinforcement Waves (per group — 3 chains total, 4 waves each)

**Wave 1** *(spawns when initial group is killed):*
- 1x Frigate — EoM Succubus
- 1x Frigate — EoM Demon *(trigger: Wave 2)*

**Wave 2** *(spawns when Demon is destroyed):*
- 2x Frigate — EoM Demon *(last kill triggers Wave 3)*

**Wave 3** *(spawns when last Demon is destroyed):*
- Left chain: 2x Frigate — EoM Succubus *(trigger: Wave 4)*
- Center & Right chains: 2x Frigate — EoM Demon *(trigger: Wave 4)*

**Wave 4:**
- 1x Cruiser — EoM Priest

## Blitz
Kill the left group of EoM ships and each subsequent spawn. Do not touch the center and right groups.

## Tactical Notes
- Frigates target drones but inflict minimal damage; T1 drone platforms (e.g., Vexor) remain viable.
- Spawns arrive in sequential chains — each initial group destroyed generates four reinforcement waves.
