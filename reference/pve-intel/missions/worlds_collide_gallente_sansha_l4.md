## Waves/Rooms

### Pocket 1 - War Zone
Gates locked until triggers killed. Can bypass with Zbikoki's Hacker Card. Groups stay within 30km of control towers.

**Gallente Group** (gate trigger: destroy Federation Navy Orion)
- 3x Frigate Federation Navy Gamma I Support
- 4x Cruiser Federation Navy Thorax
- 5x Battleship Federation Navy Dominix/Orion **[Orion = Gate Trigger]**

**Sansha Group** (gate trigger: destroy Centus Savage Lord)
- 3x Elite Frigate Centii Loyal Ravener/Scavenger (warp disrupt, web)
- 3x Battlecruiser Centatis Specter
- 3x Battleship Centus Mutant/Savage Lord **[Savage Lord = Gate Trigger]**

### Pocket 2A - Federation Base
All ships auto-aggro on warp-in.

**Initial Spawn**
- 3x Sentry Tower Gallente III
- 5x Frigate Federation Navy Soldier
- 5x Elite Frigate Federation Navy Delta II (web)
- 6x Cruiser Federation Navy Detective/Thorax (EM damage)
- 5x Battleship Federation Navy Dominix/Megathron/Orion
- 1x Battleship Republic Fleet Tempest

### Pocket 2B - Sansha Base

## Blitz
1. Pocket 1: Kill trigger battleships or use Zbikoki's Hacker Card.
2. Pocket 2 (Federation): Kill web frigates with guns/missiles, then approach acceleration gate.
3. Pocket 2 (Sansha): Clear all ships.
4. Pocket 3: Eliminate all ships, retrieve crew.

## Tactical Notes
- Two-faction mission: Gallente Federation and Sansha's Nation.
- Federation base has sentry towers; Sansha base has Spy frigates.
- Centus Plague Lords use tracking disruptors - missile ships are unaffected.
- Sansha elite frigates have both warp disrupt and web; prioritize killing them.
- Omni tank recommended with emphasis on Kinetic/Thermal.
