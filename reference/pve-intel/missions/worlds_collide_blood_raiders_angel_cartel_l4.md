## Waves/Rooms

### Pocket 1 - Warzone
Two faction groups at ~135km behind locked gates.

**Blood Raider Group** (gate trigger: destroy Corpus Prophet/Oracle)
- 3x Elite Frigate Elder Corpii Follower/Herald/Worshiper/Dark Corpii Upholder
- 2x Elite Cruiser Elder Corpum Dark Priest/Shadow Sage (NOS)
- 3x Battleship Corpus Prophet/Oracle **[Gate Trigger]**

**Angel Group** (gate trigger: destroy Gist Malakim/Throne)
- 3x Elite Frigate Arch Gistii Thug/Outlaw
- 3x Elite Cruiser Gistum Domination Crusher/Smasher
- 4x Battleship Gist Malakim/Throne **[Gate Trigger]**

### Pocket 2A - Blood Raider Base
Full aggro damage: 1,100-1,200 DPS (50% EM / 50% Thermal).

**Group 1 (Auto-Aggro, 7km Blood Watchtower)**
- 2x Frigate Blood Spy (warp disrupt, web) - shooting with guns/missiles does not aggro others
- 3x Battleship Corpus Apostle/Oracle

**Group 2 (23km, near Acceleration Gate)**
- 2x Battlecruiser Corpatis Fanatic
- 2x Battleship Corpus Apostle/Oracle

**Group 3 (24km, Blood Watchtower)**
- 5x Destroyer Corpior Friar
- 5x Battleship Corpus Apostle/Oracle

**Group 4 (7km)**
- 1x Battleship Blood Rider Supervisor
- 1x Battleship Blood Overseer (random spawn)

Mining: 31 Veldspar asteroids (47,974 m3)

### Pocket 2B - Angel Cartel Base
Group 1 auto-aggros at warp-in. Possible Gist Overlord spawn (does not aggro until attacked).

**Group 1 (Auto-Aggro, 7km)**
- 2x Frigate Angel Spy (warp disrupt, web) - drones on Spies trigger full room aggro
- 2x Battleship Blood Apostle/Oracle

**Group 2 (20km)**
- 4x Frigate Gist Viper (web)
- 2x Cruiser Gist Primus
- 3x Battleship Gist Saint (Bane torpedoes, 55km+ range)

**Group 3 (35km)**
- 3x Elite Frigate Arch Gist Hunter/Impaler
- 1x Elite Battlecruiser Arch Gist Centurion (closes to <20km)
- 4x Battleship Gist Saint/Warlord

**Group 4 (50km)**
- 5x Battleship Gist Throne/Cherubim (Havoc cruise missiles, 80km+ range)

Mining: 31 Veldspar asteroids (47,974 m3)

### Pocket 3 - Final Room (Blood Raiders)
Full aggro damage: 900-950 DPS (51% EM / 49% Thermal). Killing all ships flags mission complete.

**Group 1 (Auto-Aggro, 5km)**
- 3x Elite Battlecruiser Elder Corpum Dark Priest/Shadow Sage
- 2x Battleship Corpus Apostle/Oracle/Prophet

**Group 2 (10km)**
- 4x Elite Frigate Elder Corpii Herald/Upholder (warp disrupt, web)
- 2x Battleship Corpus Apostle/Oracle

**Group 3 (10km)**
- 3x Battlecruiser Corpatis Fanatic/Phantom
- 2x Battleship Corpus Apostle/Oracle/Prophet

## Blitz
1. Use Zbikoki's Hacker Card to bypass Pocket 1 gate lock (or kill trigger battleships).
2. Clear all ships in chosen Pocket 2 base to unlock gate.
3. In Pocket 3, destroy Group 1 to access crew. Retrieve crew and return to agent.

## Tactical Notes
- Two-faction mission: choose Blood Raider or Angel base from Pocket 1.
- Spy frigates: safe to shoot with guns/missiles; drones on Spies trigger full room aggro.
- Angel base: Gist Saints have 55km+ range; Cherubim have 80km+ range. Kill Centurion first as it closes distance fast.
- Blood Raider base: heavy EM/Thermal damage; NOS from Dark Priests drains capacitor.
- Pocket 3: defeating Group 1 yields crew pickup. Failure to collect crew results in standing loss even if mission flagged complete.
- Set drones to passive in all pockets.
- Omni tank recommended due to mixed faction damage profiles.
