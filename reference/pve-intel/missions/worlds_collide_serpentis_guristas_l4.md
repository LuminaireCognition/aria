| Field | Value |

## Waves/Rooms

### Pocket 1 - Warzone (Decision Point)
Ship groups at ~130km; gates ~20km from warp-in. Choose Serpentis or Guristas gate.

**Serpentis Group**
- 4x Elite Frigate Guardian Veteran (warp disrupt, web)
- 4x Battlecruiser Corelatis High Captain/Captain Sentry
- 4x Battleship Core High Admiral/Admiral (remote sensor damp) **[Gate Trigger]**

**Guristas Group**
- 2x Elite Cruiser Dire Pithum Abolisher (target jammer)
- 3x Battleship Pith Extinguisher/Dismantler **[Gate Trigger]**

### Pocket 2A - Serpentis Base
Group 1 and lone ships auto-aggro. Elite frigates attack drones. Gate locked until all ships destroyed.

**Group 1**
- 3x Elite Frigate Guardian Veteran (warp disrupt, web)
- 1x Battlecruiser Corelatis Platoon Leader
- 2x Battleship Core Admiral/High Admiral

**Group 2**
- 3x Elite Frigate Guardian Veteran (warp disrupt, web)
- 2x Battlecruiser Corelatis Platoon Leader
- 4x Battleship Core Admiral/High Admiral

**Group 3**
- 2x Elite Frigate Guardian Veteran (warp disrupt, web)
- 3x Battleship Core Admiral/High Admiral

**Additional NPCs**
- 1x Cruiser Serpentis Spy
- 1x Battleship Serpentis Supervisor

Serpentis Overseer may spawn after mission start. War installation drops T1 loot.

### Pocket 2B - Guristas Base
Group 1 auto-aggros. Elite frigates attack drones. Attacking lone elite frigate triggers full room. Gate locked until all ships destroyed. High incoming DPS if all groups engaged; battleships orbit at ~30km; ECM present.

**Group 1**
- 2x Elite Cruiser Dire Pithum Abolisher/Eraser (target jammer)
- 3x Battleship Pith Massacre/Extinguisher

**Group 2**
- 3x Elite Frigate Pith Usurper (warp disrupt, web)
- 5x Battleship Pith Massacre

**Group 3**
- 3x Elite Frigate Pith Invader (warp disrupt, web)
- 4x Elite Cruiser Dire Pithum Abolisher (target jammer)

**Additional NPCs**
- 1x Elite Frigate Guristas Kyoukan (warp disrupt, web)
- 1x Battleship Guristas Overseer

Overseer may appear later. Groups may be engaged separately.

### Pocket 3 - Research Outpost (Serpentis ships)
Group 1 present on arrival. Groups 2 and 3 warp in shortly after. Tag Group 1 before reinforcements arrive to distinguish them. Killing Group 1 unlocks Heron cargo hold. Contains 32 Veldspar asteroids (703,056 units).

**Group 1**
- 3x Battlecruiser Corelatis Captain Sentry
- 3x Battleship Core Vice Admiral/Admiral (remote sensor damp)

**Group 2**
- 3x Destroyer Corelior Trooper/Soldier
- 3x Battlecruiser Corelatis Captain Sentry
- 6x Battleship Core Vice Admiral/Admiral (remote sensor damp)

**Group 3**
- 3x Destroyer Corelior Trooper/Soldier
- 3x Battlecruiser Corelatis Captain Sentry
- 6x Battleship Core Vice Admiral/Admiral (remote sensor damp)

## Blitz
1. Use Zbikoki's Hacker Card to bypass Pocket 1 gate lock (or kill trigger battleships).
2. Clear all ships in chosen Pocket 2 to unlock gate.
3. In Pocket 3, kill Group 1 quickly (before Groups 2-3 warp in) to unlock Heron. Retrieve crew (40 m3) and return to agent.

## Tactical Notes
- Pure Kinetic/Thermal tank is sufficient for both factions.
- Pocket 3 has heavy remote sensor dampening from Serpentis battleships; bring sensor boosters or use drones/missiles.
- Pocket 3 Groups 2 and 3 warp in shortly after arrival - tag Group 1 immediately to track them.
- Guristas base has significant ECM (target jammers); bring ECCM modules or passive drones.
- Elite frigates attack drones in all pockets; set drones to passive.
- Attacking the lone Guristas Kyoukan triggers full room aggro.
- Occasional faction Shadow Serpentis module drops (~200M ISK).
