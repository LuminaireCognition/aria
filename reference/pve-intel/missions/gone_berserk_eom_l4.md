## Waves/Rooms

### Initial Spawn (Auto-Aggro)
- 3x Cruiser — EoM Crusader *(each triggers Wave 1 on destruction)*
- 3x Battleship — EoM Hydra

### Reinforcement Waves (per Crusader — 3 chains total, 4 waves each)

**Wave 1** *(spawns when a Crusader is destroyed):*
- 2x Cruiser — EoM Death Knight *(last one triggers Wave 2)*

**Wave 2** *(spawns when last Death Knight is destroyed):*
- 0-2x Frigate — EoM Saboteur *(Web)*
- 1x Battleship — EoM Hydra *(trigger: Wave 3)*

**Wave 3** *(spawns when Hydra is destroyed):*
- 1x Battleship — EoM Hydra
- 1x Battleship — EoM Ogre *(trigger: Wave 4)*

**Wave 4** *(spawns when Ogre is destroyed):*
- 1x Battleship — EoM Death Lord *(long-range missiles, 100km+)*

## Blitz
No blitz available — all ships across all chains must be destroyed.

## Tactical Notes
- **Set drones to passive** to avoid triggering multiple spawn chains simultaneously.
- EoM Saboteurs (frigates) have web capability.
- Elite frigates may have warp disruption — watch for tackle.
- Focus one spawn chain at a time: kill one Crusader, clear all 4 waves, then trigger the next.
- EoM Death Lord has long-range missiles effective past 100 km — close range or maintain transversal.
- Mining asteroids were removed by CCP.
